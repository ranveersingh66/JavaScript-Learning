Few facts about the world we live in

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known astronomical body to harbor life. Formed about 4.5 billion years ago, its unique atmosphere, moderate temperatures, and abundant liquid water allow diverse ecosystems to thrive.

About the Earth

  • Shape: An oblate spheroid (flattened at the poles, bulging at the equator).
  • Diameter: 12,756 km (7,926 miles).
  • Surface Area: 510.1 million km² (197 million square miles).
  • Atmosphere: Composed primarily of nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%).
  • Notable Moons: The Moon

Top three facts about the Earth

  • The Earth's rotation is gradually slowing.
  • Earth is at the center of the solar system.
  • Earth has a powerful magnetic field.
